## 🌐 About Us

At **V-Insight AI Lab**, we focus on multimodal intelligence that connects visual perception with language reasoning. Our work explores how vision-language models can ground open-ended concepts, reason over complex visual scenes, and understand dynamic video content.

Our research spans **free-form multimodal grounding**, **perception-reasoning benchmarks**, **3D spatial understanding**, and **VideoLLM training and evaluation**. We aim to build practical models, datasets, and evaluation tools that make multimodal systems more interpretable, reliable, and useful in real-world scenarios.

---

## 🚀 Core Research Tracks

### 🎯 Free-form Multimodal Grounding
*Grounding models that combine fine-grained perception with compositional reasoning.*

- 🧩 **[Motto](https://github.com/TG0110/Motto/tree/main)**: Mixture-of-Thought-Tokens, a unified framework for free-form multimodal grounding. It bridges fine-grained visual perception and semantic reasoning with spatially grounded thought tokens and adaptive reasoning tokens.

### 📊 Benchmarks & Evaluation
*Evaluation resources for diagnosing the perception-reasoning gap in multimodal grounding models.*

- 📏 **[PR-Bench](https://github.com/TG0110/PR-Bench)**: A perception-reasoning benchmark for multimodal grounding. It evaluates models across visual-cue perception and compositional reasoning scenarios, including attributes, positions, interactions, relations, commonsense, and rejection.

### 🧭 Spatial & Video Understanding
*Multimodal reasoning over 3D scenes, temporal evidence, and long-form video content.*

- 🧱 **[GeoAnchor](https://github.com/JerryPW/GeoAnchor)**: Collaborative reasoning via latent decomposition for 3D spatial understanding, enabling models to better capture geometry, spatial relations, and scene structure.
- 🎬 **[MeSD](https://github.com/WhiteVentral/MeSD-code)**: Multi-Evidence Self-Distillation for VideoLLMs, providing training and evaluation code for improving video understanding, long-video reasoning, and spatiotemporal grounding.
- ⏱️ **[DEMR](https://github.com/KaijingOfficial/DEMR)**: Adaptive evidential learning for robust temporal moment retrieval, improving video-language localization under temporal and semantic uncertainty.
- 📹 **[CLIP2Video](https://github.com/CryhanFang/CLIP2Video)**: Transferring Image-CLIP to video-text retrieval through temporal relation modeling.

### 🛡️ Robust Multimodal Learning
*Trustworthy learning methods for robust representation, alignment, and multi-view understanding.*

- 🔗 **[TUNED](https://github.com/JethroJames/TUNED)**: Trusted unified feature-neighborhood dynamics for robust multi-view classification.
